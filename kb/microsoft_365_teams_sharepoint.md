# Microsoft 365, Teams, and SharePoint Access Triage

## Teams audio or meeting issue

Confirm the correct microphone, speaker, camera, operating-system privacy permission, Teams device setting, mute state, and whether the device works in another application. Check for a known Microsoft 365 service incident and record the Teams version and test-call result.

## SharePoint access issue

Confirm the user, site and library URL, requested access level, error message, business owner, and whether access previously worked. Compare browser/private-window behaviour and confirm sign-in identity. Do not grant access without the site owner's approval; route permission changes through the approved process.

## Account and sign-in issue

Confirm identity using the approved verification method. Record the application, device, network, timestamp, and error. Never ask for or record a password or MFA code. Escalate suspected account compromise immediately.
