# Onboarding and Offboarding Checklist

## Onboarding

- Confirm the approved request, manager, start date, role, location, required applications, access owners, accessibility needs, and equipment.
- Select and record asset tags; apply approved operating-system updates, encryption, endpoint protection, and standard software.
- Route Microsoft 365, Teams, SharePoint, phone, and other permissions through the approved owners.
- Test sign-in, network, printing, audio/video, remote-support tooling, and MFA enrollment without recording credentials.
- Provide a short orientation covering hardware, core applications, security reporting, support channels, and self-help articles.
- Obtain user confirmation and update ticket, asset, and licence records.

## Offboarding

Follow the approved HR/manager request and timing. Revoke or transfer access through authorized administrators, recover and inspect equipment, preserve required data, update asset and licence records, and document completion without exposing confidential HR details.
